Sum up 23 November's world news in one image: a one-page newspaper front page for Monday 23 November 2026 — COP31 collapsing in Antalya with the world reacting, missiles over Riyadh and tankers hit at Hormuz, India six wickets from victory after its record 720–9 in Wellington, Modi meeting Nepal's PM Shah in Delhi, South Korea's education minister quitting over the AI exam-cheating row, and BepiColombo reaching Mercury with a thruster glitch, plus a ticker with Gaza, Ukraine, Lisbon and South Sudan. The image is drawn by `draw.py` (Pillow) from the day's real reporting, and `main.py` copies it to `/output/entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
